Overview

hack.todo ("the App") is a terminal-style task management application developed by irodori (irodori.developer@gmail.com).

This policy describes how the App handles your information. The short version: your data stays on your device. We collect nothing.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. The App has no backend, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored exclusively on your device and never leaves it:

  • Task titles and completion status (via SwiftData)
  • App settings: selected theme, typing speed, username (via UserDefaults)
  • Onboarding completion flag (via UserDefaults)
  • Premium unlock status (verified via Apple's StoreKit)

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App.

In-App Purchases

The App offers a one-time premium upgrade ("hack.todo Premium") processed entirely through Apple's App Store infrastructure (StoreKit 2).

  • We never receive, store, or process your payment information
  • All purchase transactions are handled by Apple
  • Apple's privacy policy applies to payment processing

For details on how Apple handles your data, see apple.com/legal/privacy.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social SDKs. No data is shared with third parties.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since no data is collected at all, the App is safe for users of all ages.

Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device and protected by iOS's built-in security mechanisms including device encryption and app sandboxing.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
